🫧 Full Disclosure — exactly how the pool works

Entries. Every valid share your miner submits is one unit. {{ENTRY_UNIT}} units make one entry, and partial units are kept, so nothing you mine is rounded away. Mining is the only way entries are earned — no purchase is required to enter a drawing.

Streak. A contributing day is a UTC day in which your miner submitted shares. On every 7th consecutive contributing day that day's shares convert at 3×; on other days they convert at 1×. The bonus changes entries only — never your hashrate.

The pot. The prize shown on the homepage is the pool's configured balance minus every prize already drawn but not yet claimed. Money is never counted twice: a claim removes the paid amount from the balance, and a prize unclaimed after {{CLAIM_DAYS}} days returns to the pot for the next drawing.

The drawing. A new drawing runs every {{DRAW_HOURS}} hours. One entry is selected from that round's recorded entries; the winner receives {{WINNER_PCT}}% of the pot and the remaining {{HOUSE_TAX_PCT}}% is the house tax, which stays in the pool and seeds the next prize. The pick is commit-reveal: the commitment is published before the draw, the seed is derived with a Monero block hash, and every drawing can be re-checked at /api/verify/{id}.

Scout Pro — the two unlocks. Pro is unlocked either by {{PRO_DAYS}} contributing days or by a one-off purchase of ${{PRO_PRICE_USD}} (≈{{PRO_PRICE_XMR}} XMR at the current rate) at checkout. That is the exact price the checkout asks for; 70% of it is credited to the jackpot and 30% is kept by the operator, once per order. Either path awards exactly one +1 entry. If your account has no payout wallet yet, the entry is held on the account (not lost) and is minted the moment a wallet is attached.

Alerts. Buy/sell alerts are served by the pool only to a signed-in account that holds Pro: the entitlement is checked on the server for every signal request, so a locked account receives no notifications regardless of what the browser sends.

How to Play: Mine Monero, Earn Entries, Win the Daily Pot

XMR Jackpot turns mining into a daily draw. You point a Monero miner at the pool, every share you contribute counts toward your next entry ({{ENTRY_UNIT}} shares buy one), and once a day one entry takes {{WINNER_PCT}}% of the pot. There is nothing to deposit and nothing to buy.

What you need to start

  • A computer that can run the miner — the bundle is on the download page.
  • No Monero wallet to start mining. You need one only to receive a prize.
  • No deposit, no entry fee, no purchase. Mining is the only way in.

Step 1 — Run the miner

Download the bundle, unzip it, and start the miner. It connects to the pool and begins submitting shares. There is no wallet address to enter before you start and no configuration file to edit.

Download the miner

Step 2 — Every {{ENTRY_UNIT}} shares is one entry

The pool counts accepted shares, not hours online. For every {{ENTRY_UNIT}} shares your miner submits, you receive one free entry in the drawing that is currently open. Entries stay with the round until it closes, so steady mining compounds: the longer you contribute, the more entries you hold when the draw runs.

Shares are credited as the pool accepts them, so a dashboard that trails your miner by a few submissions is normal. The total is unaffected.

Step 3 — The drawing runs every {{DRAW_HOURS}} hours

Each drawing closes {{DRAW_HOURS}} hours after the previous one. One entry is selected from the entries recorded for that round, and the winner takes {{WINNER_PCT}}% of the pot. The remaining {{HOUSE_TAX_PCT}}% covers servers and development, and seeds the next pot when it runs short.

The pot carries over rather than resetting to nothing: an unclaimed prize returns to the pool and grows the next drawing.

Step 4 — Claim within seven days

If your entry is drawn, log in, connect a Monero wallet, and claim the prize. You have seven days. After that the prize returns to the pool for the next drawing, so connecting a wallet before you win is the safer order of operations.

How the draw stays verifiable

The pool commits to a seed before the drawing and reveals it afterwards; the seed is derived from Monero block hashes, so neither the operator nor a miner can pick a winner after seeing the entries.
