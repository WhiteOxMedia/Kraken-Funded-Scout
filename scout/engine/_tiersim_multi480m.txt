NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  · the detector sees the last 720 bars at every step, exactly
    as the scanner feeds it; a longer window widens the EVIDENCE,
    never the detector's own 5m / 24-bar (2h) coil contract.
  · two views of every arm, same trades up to the point a book
    stops: CONTINUOUS runs the window out (what the edge is),
    CONTEST halts the book the moment it reaches +12% or the death
    line (what the rules would have done). A 'touched +12%' book
    is a PASS: the contest ends when the goal is reached, so what the
    continuous view shows afterwards is a book that declined to stop,
    not a pass that was taken back.
  · more time is not an open-ended position: the clock still
    calls the exit on the first bar past the hold window, so a trade
    that never reaches the goal is closed at the window's price.
  · the book holds several positions at once, one per pair, so
    a long hold no longer starves entries; each entry is sized
    against the FREE CASH still on the book, so the account can
    never be over-committed. The 3% guard sizes every entry and
    the fee is charged on both legs. Champion/mover funnels are
    not replayable.
